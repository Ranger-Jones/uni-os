## What's new

- **Crop images in your notes.** Select an image in a note and click the new **Crop** button in its toolbar. Drag the frame or its handles, then press **Crop** (or Enter). Undo brings the original back.
- **Figures from lecture PDFs, inserted by Claude.** Claude Desktop and Claude Code (via MCP) can now look at the pages of your course PDFs and cut figures, diagrams and tables straight into a note — for example: *"Illustrate my notes on scene graphs with the figures from the lecture slides."* Images keep their display width when notes are read and rewritten by the assistant.

Thanks to our first outside contributor for this feature!
