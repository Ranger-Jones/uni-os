## What's new

- **"On which slide is this?"** The assistant and the study buddy can now tell you which file and slide a topic is on — just ask, e.g. *"Where are the cross product and the line at infinity explained?"*. Your existing PDFs are read once more in the background after the update, so they get slide numbers too.
- **Better answers from local models.** Local models (Ollama, LM Studio) now look up your lecture slides instead of guessing, search with the English terms of the slides when you ask in German, and try again on their own when a search finds nothing.

## Improvements

- Updated libraries (Anthropic SDK 1.2, PDF rendering).
