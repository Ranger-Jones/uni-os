## What's new

- **Meet your study buddy.** A little dancing monkey now lives in the bottom-right corner. Click it and ask about the page you are on — "What is this about?", "Quiz me on this page", "What is due in this course?". It reads the open note, course or quiz and can look up anything else in UniOS. In **Settings → Study buddy** you can pick another animal (cat, dog, panda, fox, penguin, frog or owl), give it a name and a personality, choose how much it dances, its size and which model answers.
- **Use a local AI model.** The assistant (and the study buddy) can now run on your own computer with **Ollama** or **LM Studio** — free and private, no API key. Choose **Local model** in **Settings → Assistant**, click **Check connection** and pick a model with tool support, such as `qwen3:14b`.
- **Zoom your notes.** Make note text bigger or smaller (50–150 %) with ⌘+ / ⌘− (Ctrl on Windows and Linux), a trackpad pinch, or the slider in the page menu. ⌘0 resets it.
- **Reading progress.** While you scroll through a long note, a small indicator shows how far you are.

## Improvements

- The assistant finds graded quizzes and exams in your calendar when you ask what is due.
- When the assistant asks for a course that does not exist, it now gets a clear error and corrects itself instead of reporting "nothing found".
- Updated formula rendering (KaTeX 0.19.1).

Thanks to our contributor for note zoom and reading progress!
