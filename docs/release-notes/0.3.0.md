## What's new

- **Quizzes and test exams.** Every course has a new **Tests** tab. A *quiz* gives you feedback after every question; a *test exam* is a mock exam with points, an optional time limit and a grade estimate (1.0–5.0) at the end.
- **Generated from your notes.** Click **Generate with AI**, pick the pages and materials, choose how many questions and which types — single and multiple choice, true/false, numeric results and open answers. Review the proposals, untick what you don't want, save.
- **Open answers are graded for you.** Write explanations or derivations in your own words; the assistant grades them against the model answer with points and short feedback. You can always change the points yourself.
- **Learn from mistakes.** Every attempt is kept with your answers, so you see your best and latest score, review each question with its solution and explanation, and use **Retry mistakes** to practise only what you got wrong.
- **Old exams.** **Add test exam** keeps an old exam together with its PDFs from *Materials*, and **Record a paper result** saves the points you reached on paper.
- **Collapsible sidebar.** Hide the sidebar with the button at its top or with ⌘\ / Ctrl+\ — notes, courses and the Today view then use the extra width.
- **Assistant and Claude Desktop.** Quizzes and exams are in search (⌘K / Ctrl+K), and the assistant (and Claude Desktop via MCP) can create tests, add questions and look at your results.

| macOS – Apple Silicon (M1–M4) | `UniOS-0.3.0-arm64.dmg` |

| Windows 10/11 | `UniOS-Setup-0.3.0-x64.exe` |
| Linux | `UniOS-0.3.0-x86_64.AppImage` or `UniOS-0.3.0-amd64.deb` |
